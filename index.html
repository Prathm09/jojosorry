First, the most important thing

I wish I could take that moment back, JOJO.

I have replayed things in my head, wishing I had chosen better words, listened more carefully, or handled things differently. I cannot rewind that moment, and I know saying “sorry” doesn't erase how it made you feel.

If I hurt you, disappointed you, or made you feel like your feelings didn't matter, I am genuinely sorry. You deserved patience, respect, and care from me — especially in a moment when I failed to give you that.

“I can't change what I did, but I can take responsibility for it and choose to do better.”
🐼🫶
Please know this

You matter to me.

I may not always have the right words, but that doesn't make your place in my life any less important. I never want you to feel easy to lose, easy to ignore, or someone whose feelings can be brushed aside.

I'm sorry for the moment I made you upset.
I'm sorry if I didn't understand how you were feeling.
I'm sorry if my ego, carelessness, or silence spoke louder than my friendship.

I won't promise to become perfect overnight. I can promise something more real: I'll listen without getting defensive, respect what you feel, own my mistakes, and work to make sure this doesn't happen again.

What I wish I had said then:

“JOJO, I hear you. I understand that I hurt you, and I'm not going to dismiss it or make excuses. You matter to me, and I'm sorry I didn't show that properly when it mattered.”

I am not asking you to forget. I'm asking you to believe that I want to learn from this.

🐼🌷
A few little reminders

Our friendship is precious.

Friendship doesn't mean two people will never hurt or misunderstand each other. It means both people deserve honesty, respect, and the chance to be heard. I miss the ease of being okay with you, but I know trust is rebuilt through actions, not pretty paragraphs.

😂The random laughs
🤍The comfort of being us
🌈The little memories
🫂Having each other's back

Those little things matter because they are part of a friendship I truly value. And JOJO, you matter — not because I need you to forgive me, but because you are you.

📏💗
An extremely scientific test

How sorry am I?

No number can measure an apology, and I know a cute little meter can't repair anything. Still, let me show you how much I wish I had handled things differently.

Measuring from the bottom of my heart...
🐼🧸
No pressure, just honesty

Can we talk when you're ready?

If you ever feel ready to talk, I want to hear your side properly. I won't interrupt, turn it into an argument, or make you comfort me. I want to understand what hurt you, even if hearing it is difficult.

You do not owe me an immediate reply, a smile, or forgiveness. Take the time you need. I will respect your space, because an apology should never become another burden for the person who was hurt.

“I miss the comfort of our friendship. I hope we can find our way back, one honest conversation and one better choice at a time.”

From me, to you

Will you forgive me, JOJO?

You don't have to choose the answer that makes me happy. Choose what is true for you. If you need time, I will understand.

Either answer is okay. I just wanted you to know I'm sorry.

A little hope for a new page

Thank you, JOJO.

If you choose to forgive me, I will never treat that kindness casually. I'll be grateful for the chance to rebuild your trust slowly, through the way I speak, listen, and show up.

And if you're not ready, that's okay too. I won't use this page, our memories, or my feelings to pressure you. Your hurt deserves room, and your choice deserves respect.

You deserved better from me in that moment. I'm sorry, JOJO. I mean that — without excuses, and without expecting anything in return.
